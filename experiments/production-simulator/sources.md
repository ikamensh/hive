# Engineering references and provenance

Reviewed 2026-09-05. Sources support relationships and model boundaries; all shipped benchmark material, machine, price, defect, and reliability numbers are synthetic unless a future dataset explicitly says otherwise.

| Source | Used for | Implementation boundary |
|---|---|---|
| [MIT 2.008, Spring 2025, Homework 1, Appendix I, p.16](https://ocw.mit.edu/courses/2-008-design-and-manufacturing-ii-spring-2025/mit2_008_s25_hw1.pdf) | Plane-wall cooling equation, diffusivity units, geometry dependence | Use the `4/pi` center-temperature first-term formula; `h` is full thickness. The brief's numerical cases are calculated from synthetic inputs, not copied MIT exercise answers. |
| [BASF Injection Molding Cycle Time Estimator](https://pmtools-na.basf.com/quickcost/cycletime.html) | Explicit warning that a thermal cycle estimate requires real process validation | BASF's online estimator discusses average-temperature cooling. Do not mix that criterion with this simulator's center-temperature formula. Its linked [cooling-time technical note](https://pmtools-na.basf.com/quickcost/cooling_time_est.pdf) discusses uniform-temperature, fixed-wall, effective-diffusivity approximations. |
| [Autodesk Moldflow: Clamp force](https://help.autodesk.com/cloudhelp/2014/ENU/MoldflowAdvisor/files/GUID-73BF9FA2-9CBE-46F4-B324-58FB7EB6E853.htm) | Pressure acting on projected area; filling and packing both matter | Use average cavity pressure as a scenario input. The simulator does not calculate the spatial pressure field. Include all cavities and the runner projection. |
| [ENGEL: Tips for process optimisation in injection moulding](https://www.engelglobal.com/en/gb/blog/tips-for-process-optimisation-in-injection-moulding) | Plasticizing can use the cooling period; parallel movements affect cycle time | The brief defines a specific overlap convention to make timing unambiguous. It is not a complete controller model for every press. |
| [BASF: Injection-Molding Problems in Engineering Thermoplastics, pp.14–19 and 28–29](https://download.basf.com/p1/8a8082587fd4b608017fd6631d5a24b1/en/Injection-Molding_Problems_in_Engineering_Thermoplastics_-_Causes_and_Solutions) | Holding conditions, inadequate cooling, and clamp/cavity pressure can affect defects | Only qualitative tendencies are source-supported. Probability curves and thresholds must be labeled synthetic. No real yield/warpage accuracy claim is supported. |
| [Vorne: OEE Factors](https://www.oee.com/oee-factors/) | Availability, performance, quality, and treatment of changeover losses | OEE is scoped to a stated planned observation window and reference ideal cycle. Factory comparisons primarily use throughput, delivery, mass, energy, and cost. |
| [NIST: Simantha—Simulation for Manufacturing](https://www.nist.gov/services-resources/software/simantha-simulation-manufacturing) | Discrete-event manufacturing, finite buffers, maintenance, and repeated stochastic comparisons | Architectural precedent only; adopting Simantha is not required. A small explicit event queue may fit this initial product better. |

Conservation equations, deterministic replay rules, order-allocation policy, event tie ordering, and economic accounting are this project's explicit modeling contracts. They are not attributed to any source as measured facts. Real calibration would require material-specific effective properties, cavity pressure/temperature measurements, cycle traces, quality samples, failure histories, and hold-out scenarios.
